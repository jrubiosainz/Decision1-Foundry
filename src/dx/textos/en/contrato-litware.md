# “Litware CRM” SaaS subscription agreement

Madrid, 2 March 2026.

## Parties

Of the one part, **Fabrikam, S.A.**, with tax ID (NIF) A00000001 and registered address at Calle Ficticia 12, 28000 Madrid, represented by Ms Elena Ejemplo Martín, Chief Financial Officer (hereinafter “Fabrikam” or “the Customer”).

Of the other part, **Litware Cloud, S.L.**, with tax ID (NIF) B00000002 and registered address at Avenida Imaginaria 45, 28000 Madrid, represented by Mr Pablo Muestra Ortega, Chief Executive Officer (hereinafter “Litware” or “the Supplier”).

Both parties enter into this contract (the “Contract”) in accordance with the following

## Clauses

### Clause 1. Subject matter

1.1. Litware shall provide Fabrikam with access to the Litware CRM platform, “Business” edition (the “Service”), for forty (40) named users, with the contacts, opportunities, forecasting and reporting modules.

1.2. The Service includes implementation, the initial migration of the contacts provided by Fabrikam, user training (two four-hour sessions) and email support on working days from 9:00 to 18:00.

### Clause 2. Term and renewal

2.1. The Contract shall enter into force on 1 April 2026 and shall have an initial term of thirty-six (36) months.

2.2. On expiry, the Contract shall renew automatically for successive periods of thirty-six (36) months, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and eighty (180) days before the end of the current period.

2.3. During the initial term and each renewal, neither party may terminate the Contract for convenience or withdraw from it without cause. The fees for the entire current period shall be payable even if Fabrikam stops using the Service.

### Clause 3. Price

3.1. The price of the Service is €28,000 per subscription year, that is, €84,000 for the initial term. Amounts are stated in euros and exclude VAT, which shall be charged at the applicable rate.

3.2. The price includes the implementation, migration and training described in clause 1.2, with no additional set-up cost.

3.3. Fabrikam may add users at any time at €48 per user per month. There is no minimum commitment for additional users or usage.

3.4. Additional professional services shall require a quote accepted in writing by Fabrikam, and travel expenses shall require its prior written approval. Litware shall not charge any surcharges or commissions on third-party costs.

### Clause 4. Price review

4.1. Litware may unilaterally increase the annual price of the Service by up to twelve per cent (12%) for each subscription year, without linking it to any index, by notifying Fabrikam by email thirty (30) days in advance.

4.2. A price review shall not entitle Fabrikam to terminate the Contract.

### Clause 5. Invoicing and payment

5.1. Litware shall invoice one hundred per cent (100%) of the annual fee in advance at the start of each subscription year.

5.2. Fabrikam shall pay each invoice by bank transfer within fifteen (15) calendar days of its issue date.

5.3. Invoices shall state Fabrikam's purchase order number and itemise the period, users and charges.

5.4. Late payments shall accrue late-payment interest under Spanish Law 3/2004 of 29 December, with no other penalties. Litware shall not require any bank guarantee, deposit or other security from Fabrikam.

5.5. If the Contract is terminated for breach by Litware, Litware shall refund to Fabrikam the pro rata portion of the annual fee paid and not used.

### Clause 6. Service levels

6.1. Litware shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the platform available 99.5% of the time each month, excluding scheduled downtime.

6.2. This target is for guidance only: failure to meet it shall not give rise to service credits, discounts, penalties or any compensation for Fabrikam.

### Clause 7. Supplier obligations

7.1. Litware warrants that the Service will operate in accordance with its documentation and shall not change its terms or reduce its functionality without Fabrikam's written agreement.

7.2. Litware appoints its account manager, Ms Irene Prueba Sanz, as Fabrikam's point of contact.

7.3. Litware shall comply with applicable law, including anti-corruption rules, and shall not offer or accept improper payments.

7.4. Throughout the term of the Contract, Litware shall maintain civil liability and cyber-risk insurance with minimum cover of €1,000,000 per claim.

### Clause 8. Intellectual property

8.1. Litware retains all rights in the platform; Fabrikam receives a non-exclusive right of use for the term of the Contract.

8.2. Configurations, templates, reports and other deliverables created specifically for Fabrikam, as well as its data, shall belong exclusively to Fabrikam.

8.3. Litware shall defend and hold Fabrikam harmless against third-party claims for infringement of intellectual or industrial property rights arising from the use of the Service.

### Clause 9. Confidentiality

9.1. Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only to perform the Contract. This obligation shall remain in force for the term of the Contract and for five (5) years after its termination.

### Clause 10. Data protection: data processing agreement

10.1. To provide the Service, Litware shall process on Fabrikam's behalf the contact details of Fabrikam's customers and prospects (name, company, job title, email, telephone and sales history). Fabrikam is the controller and Litware the processor, under Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

10.2. Litware shall process the data only on Fabrikam's documented instructions and shall not use it for its own purposes, such as improving its products, compiling statistics, advertising or training models.

10.3. Litware's authorised staff have committed in writing to confidentiality and receive regular training in data protection and security.

10.4. Litware shall assist Fabrikam in responding to data subjects' requests and with impact assessments and prior consultations with the supervisory authority. Its data protection officer can be contacted at dpd@litware.example.

### Clause 11. Information security

11.1. Litware shall apply the measures of Article 32 GDPR: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control with two-factor authentication, daily backups and access logging.

11.2. Litware holds ISO/IEC 27001 certification and shall provide Fabrikam each year with a copy of the current certificate and a summary of the external audit report.

### Clause 12. Data location, sub-processors and security breaches

12.1. Fabrikam's data shall be hosted in the data centres of Litware Hosting Inc., a Litware affiliate, located in the state of Virginia (United States). Litware Hosting Inc. is not certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Fabrikam authorises this international transfer without the need to sign standard contractual clauses or any other additional instrument.

12.2. Litware may engage new sub-processors at any time, without Fabrikam's prior authorisation or advance notice, and Fabrikam may not object to them.

12.3. Litware shall notify Fabrikam without undue delay of any personal data breaches of which it becomes aware.

### Clause 13. Termination and return of data

13.1. Either party may terminate the Contract if the other seriously breaches its obligations and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

13.2. On termination of the Contract for any reason, access to the platform shall be deactivated and Litware shall delete all of Fabrikam's data, including backups, within fifteen (15) days. Litware shall not be obliged to return the data or to enable its export.

### Clause 14. Liability

14.1. Litware's total liability on any grounds shall not exceed the fees for the last month of service (€2,333.33).

14.2. This limitation shall not apply to damage caused by wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

### Clause 15. Force majeure

15.1. Neither party shall be liable for failures caused by force majeure for as long as it lasts.

### Clause 16. Assignment, exclusivity and amendments

16.1. Litware may not assign the Contract without Fabrikam's prior written consent.

16.2. The Contract does not grant exclusivity: Fabrikam may contract similar services from other suppliers.

16.3. Any amendment to the Contract shall require a written agreement signed by both parties, except for the price review provided for in clause 4.

### Clause 17. Notices

17.1. Notices shall be sent in writing to the addresses given at the head of this Contract or by email with acknowledgement of receipt to contratos@fabrikam.example and legal@litware.example.

### Clause 18. Governing law and jurisdiction

18.1. The Contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware (United States of America).

18.2. For any dispute, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts of the State of Delaware.

In witness whereof, the parties sign two copies.
