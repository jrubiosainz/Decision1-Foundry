# Marketing and creative services agreement

Madrid, 20 January 2026.

## Parties

Of the one part, **Fabrikam, S.A.**, with tax ID (NIF) A00000001 and registered address at Calle Ficticia 12, 28000 Madrid, represented by Ms Elena Ejemplo Martín, Chief Financial Officer (hereinafter “Fabrikam” or “the Customer”).

Of the other part, **Lucerne Media, S.L.**, with tax ID (NIF) B00000004 and registered address at Paseo Ficticio 77, 28000 Madrid, represented by Ms Carla Prototipo León, Managing Director (hereinafter “Lucerne” or “the Agency”).

Both parties enter into this contract (the “Contract”) in accordance with the following

## Clauses

### Clause 1. Subject matter

1.1. Lucerne shall provide Fabrikam with the following services (the “Services”):

- **Ongoing marketing service:** annual strategic plan, management of three social media profiles, a monthly email newsletter and a monthly results report.
- **Four seasonal campaigns** (spring, summer, back to school and Christmas), each with a creative concept, graphic assets, a video of up to 30 seconds and the planning and buying of digital media.

1.2. Each campaign shall be developed from a brief approved by Fabrikam, which shall validate each deliverable in writing.

### Clause 2. Term

2.1. The Contract shall enter into force on 1 February 2026 and shall last twelve (12) months, at the end of which it shall expire without automatic renewal. Any extension shall require the written agreement of both parties.

### Clause 3. Termination

3.1. Lucerne may terminate the Contract at any time, without giving reasons, by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

3.2. Fabrikam may not terminate the Contract for convenience before its expiry. It may only terminate it if Lucerne seriously breaches its obligations and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

### Clause 4. Exclusivity

4.1. For twenty-four (24) months from the signing of the Contract, Fabrikam may not contract marketing, advertising, creative or media-buying services from other agencies or suppliers.

### Clause 5. Price

5.1. The price of the Services is €150,000, excluding VAT, broken down as follows:

- ongoing service: €7,500 per month for twelve months (€90,000);
- campaigns: €15,000 per campaign, four campaigns (€60,000).

5.2. The price is fixed for the entire term of the Contract and shall not be subject to review. It includes the strategic plan and the initial onboarding of Fabrikam's team, with no additional set-up cost. All amounts are stated in euros.

5.3. Out-of-scope work shall be invoiced at €85 per hour, subject to Fabrikam's prior written acceptance of the quote. Production expenses (photography, models or locations) shall require Fabrikam's prior written approval.

### Clause 6. Media spend

6.1. Media spend (search engine, social media and digital press advertising) is not included in the price and is decided by Fabrikam for each campaign. The Contract does not set any minimum spend.

6.2. Lucerne shall invoice media spend at cost, plus a 15% management commission on the media spend.

### Clause 7. Invoicing and payment

7.1. On signing the Contract, Lucerne shall invoice fifty per cent (50%) of the price, that is, €75,000, as an advance payment.

7.2. The remaining 50% (€75,000) shall be invoiced in twelve monthly instalments of €6,250, in arrears. Media spend and its commission shall be invoiced at the close of each campaign.

7.3. Invoices shall state Fabrikam's purchase order number and itemise the charges by service, campaign and period. They shall be paid by bank transfer thirty (30) days from their date.

7.4. Late payments shall accrue late-payment interest under Spanish Law 3/2004 of 29 December, with no other penalties. No bank guarantee, deposit or other security is required from Fabrikam.

7.5. If the Contract ends before its expiry because of a breach by Lucerne or because Lucerne terminates it, Lucerne shall refund to Fabrikam the part of the advance payment corresponding to the Services not provided.

### Clause 8. Service levels

8.1. Given the creative nature of the Services, the parties expressly agree that no measurable service levels or penalties are established. Quality is ensured through Fabrikam's approval of each deliverable, which Lucerne shall revise up to twice at no cost.

### Clause 9. Team, compliance and insurance

9.1. Lucerne appoints Ms Sofía Ensayo Paredes as account director and Fabrikam's point of contact.

9.2. Lucerne shall comply with applicable law, including advertising and consumer protection legislation and anti-corruption rules.

9.3. Lucerne shall maintain professional civil liability insurance with minimum cover of €600,000 per claim.

### Clause 10. Intellectual property

10.1. Lucerne shall retain ownership of all intellectual property rights in the concepts, designs, copy, videos and other creative assets produced in performing the Contract, including those created specifically for Fabrikam.

10.2. Lucerne grants Fabrikam a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use those assets in Spain for twelve (12) months from their delivery. Any later use shall require a new licence.

10.3. Lucerne warrants the originality of the assets and shall hold Fabrikam harmless against any third-party claim for infringement of intellectual property or image rights.

### Clause 11. Confidentiality

11.1. The parties shall keep each other's information confidential during the term of the Contract and for three (3) years after its termination.

### Clause 12. Data protection

12.1. To run the campaigns, Lucerne shall process data of Fabrikam's customers on Fabrikam's behalf (name, email, telephone, purchase history and preferences). Fabrikam is the controller and Lucerne the processor under Article 28 GDPR.

12.2. Lucerne shall process the data only on Fabrikam's documented instructions and shall not use it for its own purposes or for other clients. Its staff have given confidentiality undertakings and receive training in data protection and security.

12.3. The data shall be hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Fabrikam authorises Lucerne Analytics Ltd., based in London (United Kingdom), as a sub-processor to measure the campaigns; the transfer is covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

12.4. Lucerne shall notify Fabrikam of any new sub-processor thirty (30) days in advance, and Fabrikam may object.

12.5. Lucerne shall notify Fabrikam of any data security breach within a maximum of seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

12.6. Lucerne shall assist Fabrikam in responding to data subjects' requests and with impact assessments and consultations with the supervisory authority. Its data protection officer can be contacted at dpd@lucerne.example.

### Clause 13. Information security and audit

13.1. Lucerne shall apply the measures of Article 32 GDPR: encryption of data in transit and at rest, role-based access control with two-factor authentication and access logging.

13.2. Lucerne is certified to ISO/IEC 27001. Fabrikam may audit it once a year or request its audit reports.

### Clause 14. Return of data

14.1. On termination of the Contract, Lucerne shall return the data to Fabrikam in CSV format and delete it within a maximum of thirty (30) days, issuing a certificate of deletion.

### Clause 15. Liability

15.1. Lucerne's total liability is limited to the amount of twelve (12) months of fees (€150,000). This limit shall not apply to damage caused by wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

### Clause 16. Force majeure, assignment and amendments

16.1. Neither party shall be liable for failures caused by force majeure for as long as it lasts.

16.2. Lucerne may not assign the Contract without Fabrikam's prior written consent.

16.3. Any amendment to the Contract shall require a written agreement signed by both parties.

### Clause 17. Notices

17.1. Notices shall be sent in writing to the addresses given at the head of this Contract or by email with acknowledgement of receipt to contratos@fabrikam.example and contratos@lucerne.example.

### Clause 18. Governing law and jurisdiction

18.1. The Contract is governed by Spanish law. For any dispute, the parties submit to the Courts and Tribunals of the city of Madrid.

In witness whereof, the parties sign two copies.
