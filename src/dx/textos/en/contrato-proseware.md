# Office cleaning and maintenance services agreement

Madrid, 16 February 2026.

## Parties

Of the one part, **Fabrikam, S.A.**, with tax ID (NIF) A00000001 and registered address at Calle Ficticia 12, 28000 Madrid, represented by Ms Elena Ejemplo Martín, Chief Financial Officer (hereinafter “Fabrikam” or “the Customer”).

Of the other part, **Proseware Servicios Integrales, S.L.**, with tax ID (NIF) B00000003 and registered address at Calle Inventada 8, 28000 Madrid, represented by Mr Andrés Modelo Vidal, Managing Director (hereinafter “Proseware” or “the Supplier”).

Both parties enter into this contract (the “Contract”) in accordance with the following

## Clauses

### Clause 1. Subject matter

1.1. Proseware shall provide the following services (the “Services”) at Fabrikam's two sites in Madrid:

- **Head office** (Calle Ficticia 12, 2,400 m²): daily cleaning from Monday to Friday, preventive and minor corrective maintenance of electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning systems, and reception with access control from 8:00 to 19:00.
- **Warehouse** (Calle Ejemplo 30, 1,100 m²): cleaning three days a week and monthly preventive maintenance.

1.2. Tasks and frequencies are set out in the attached work plan, which forms part of the Contract.

### Clause 2. Term and termination

2.1. The Contract shall enter into force on 1 March 2026 and shall last twenty-four (24) months. It shall not renew automatically: any extension shall require the express written agreement of both parties.

2.2. Either party may terminate the Contract at any time, without giving reasons, by giving sixty (60) days' written notice, without any penalty or compensation.

2.3. Either party may terminate the Contract if the other seriously breaches its obligations and fails to remedy the breach within fifteen (15) days of written notice.

### Clause 3. Price

3.1. The price of the Services is €4,500 per month, excluding VAT (€2,800 for the head office and €1,700 for the warehouse). The estimated total amount for the twenty-four months is €108,000, excluding VAT. All amounts are stated in euros.

3.2. The price includes staff, machinery, products and consumables, the start-up of the service and the initial training of staff at the sites. Proseware shall not charge any surcharges, commissions or margins on materials or third-party costs, and no other expenses shall be reimbursed without Fabrikam's prior written authorisation.

3.3. Extraordinary work not included in the work plan shall be invoiced at €24 per hour per operative, provided that Fabrikam has accepted the quote in writing beforehand. The Contract does not oblige Fabrikam to any minimum volume of hours or additional purchases.

### Clause 4. Price review

4.1. The price shall be fixed for the first twelve (12) months. From the second year onwards it may be updated in line with the annual change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) published by the Spanish National Statistics Institute (INE), up to a maximum of three per cent (3%).

### Clause 5. Invoicing and payment

5.1. Proseware shall invoice the Services provided monthly in arrears. There are no advance payments.

5.2. Invoices shall state Fabrikam's purchase order number and break down the amount by site and by service.

5.3. Fabrikam shall pay each invoice by bank transfer sixty (60) days from receipt.

5.4. Late payments shall accrue late-payment interest under Spanish Law 3/2004 of 29 December, with no other penalties. No bank guarantee, deposit or other security is required from Fabrikam.

### Clause 6. Service levels and penalties

6.1. Proseware undertakes to: (a) respond to urgent incidents within a maximum of four (4) hours; (b) resolve non-urgent incidents within a maximum of forty-eight (48) hours; and (c) score at least 90% in the monthly quality inspections carried out with Fabrikam.

6.2. Each failure to meet these levels shall give rise to a penalty of 2% of the monthly fee, to be deducted from the following invoice, up to a maximum of 10% of the monthly fee.

### Clause 7. Staff, service manager and compliance

7.1. Proseware's staff shall report exclusively to Proseware, which shall assume all employment, Social Security and occupational health and safety obligations, and shall hold Fabrikam harmless against any claim in this respect.

7.2. Proseware appoints Ms Marta Ficción Robles as service manager and Fabrikam's point of contact.

7.3. Proseware shall comply with applicable law, including anti-corruption rules, and shall not offer or accept improper payments.

### Clause 8. Non-solicitation of staff

8.1. During the term of the Contract and for twelve (12) months after its termination, Fabrikam may not hire, directly or indirectly, any Proseware employee who has provided the Services, except in the cases of transfer of staff provided for in the applicable collective agreement.

### Clause 9. Subcontracting and assignment

9.1. Proseware may not subcontract the Services, in whole or in part, without Fabrikam's prior written consent.

9.2. Proseware may not assign the Contract without Fabrikam's prior written consent.

### Clause 10. Insurance and liability

10.1. Proseware shall maintain civil liability insurance with minimum cover of €1,500,000 per claim and shall provide Fabrikam with the certificate of the policy in force.

10.2. Proseware's total liability is limited to the amount paid by Fabrikam in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim (€54,000). This limit shall not apply to damage caused by wilful misconduct or gross negligence, to breaches of data protection obligations or to personal injury.

### Clause 11. Intellectual property and confidentiality

11.1. Work plans, reports and other documentation prepared specifically for Fabrikam shall belong to Fabrikam.

11.2. Proseware warrants that the products, methods and software it uses do not infringe third-party rights and shall hold Fabrikam harmless against any claim for infringement of intellectual or industrial property.

11.3. Proseware shall keep confidential the Fabrikam information to which it has access, during the term of the Contract and for three (3) years after its termination.

### Clause 12. Data protection

12.1. For access control purposes, Proseware shall process on Fabrikam's behalf the data of employees and visitors at the head office (first name, surnames, company, access card number and entry and exit times). No biometric data or other special categories of data are processed. Fabrikam is the controller and Proseware the processor under Article 28 GDPR.

12.2. Proseware shall process the data only on Fabrikam's documented instructions and shall not use it for its own purposes. Its staff have given a written confidentiality undertaking and receive annual training in data protection and security.

12.3. The data shall be stored on servers located in Spain and shall not be transferred outside the European Economic Area.

12.4. Proseware shall not engage sub-processors without Fabrikam's prior written authorisation.

12.5. Proseware shall notify Fabrikam of any data security breach within a maximum of forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it.

12.6. Proseware shall assist Fabrikam in responding to data subjects' requests and with impact assessments and consultations with the supervisory authority. Its data protection officer can be contacted at dpd@proseware.example.

### Clause 13. Information security and audit

13.1. Proseware shall apply the measures of Article 32 GDPR: encryption of data in transit and at rest, access restricted to authorised staff with named user accounts and profiles, and retention of access logs for a maximum of thirty (30) days.

13.2. Proseware is certified to ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Fabrikam may audit compliance with the Contract, itself or through an independent auditor, with fifteen (15) days' notice.

### Clause 14. Return and deletion of data

14.1. On termination of the Contract, Proseware shall deliver all personal data to Fabrikam in CSV format and, once receipt has been confirmed, shall delete it within a maximum of thirty (30) days, issuing a certificate of destruction.

### Clause 15. Force majeure

15.1. Neither party shall be liable for failures caused by force majeure for as long as it lasts.

### Clause 16. Exclusivity and amendments

16.1. The Contract does not grant exclusivity: Fabrikam may contract similar services from other suppliers.

16.2. Any amendment to the Contract or to the Services shall require a written agreement signed by both parties.

### Clause 17. Notices

17.1. Notices shall be sent in writing to the addresses given at the head of this Contract or by email with acknowledgement of receipt to contratos@fabrikam.example and contratos@proseware.example.

### Clause 18. Governing law and jurisdiction

18.1. The Contract is governed by Spanish law. For any dispute, the parties submit to the Courts and Tribunals of the city of Madrid.

In witness whereof, the parties sign two copies.
