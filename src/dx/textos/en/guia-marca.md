# Contoso Viajes brand guidelines: social media ads and banners

Version 3.0 · In force since September 2026 · Owner: Brand and Communications team

## 1. Purpose

Contoso Viajes is a Spanish travel agency with offices in Madrid and online sales. These guidelines set out the rules that every advertising creative for the brand (social media ads, stories and banners) must meet before it is published, whether it is produced by the in-house team or by an external agency. Each asset is reviewed against these guidelines and receives one of three decisions: publish, fix or legal (section 10).

## 2. Personality and tone

The brand is warm, approachable and optimistic. We address readers informally (using «tú» in Spanish), write short headlines of two to six words that name the destination, and avoid empty superlatives and repeated exclamation marks. Photographs show real destinations, in natural light and without heavy retouching.

## 3. Logo

3.1. The logo consists of a symbol, a teal circle (#0B5563) with a white wave inside it, followed by the name «Contoso Viajes».

3.2. The name is written in navy (#12263A) on light backgrounds, or in white on dark photographs and colour bands. The circle is always teal and the wave always white.

3.3. Only these official versions exist, in teal, white and navy. It is forbidden to:

- stretch, squash or distort the logo: its original proportions must always be kept;
- rotate or tilt it;
- change its colours or reproduce it in colours other than the official ones;
- add shadows, outlines, gradients, glows or other effects to it;
- crop it or cover part of it with other elements.

3.4. A clear space at least equal to the height of the circle must be left around the logo. Its minimum width is 120 pixels in the square format.

3.5. The logo appears exactly once in each creative, preferably in one of the top corners. A creative without the logo cannot be published.

## 4. Colour palette

| Colour | Code | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Teal | #0B5563 | Primary colour: logo symbol, bands and brand backgrounds. |
| Coral | #FF6B4A | Accent: prices, discounts and buttons. |
| Cream | #FFF4E6 | Backgrounds, bands and light text on dark backgrounds. |
| Navy | #12263A | Text and dark bands. |

White is allowed for text on dark backgrounds, for button text and for the white version of the logo.

Neon or fluorescent colours, magenta or purple gradients and any off-palette colour are forbidden in text, captions, bands, buttons or graphic elements. The natural colours of the background photograph do not count for this rule.

## 5. Typography

A single sans-serif family is used: Contoso Sans or, failing that, Helvetica or Arial. The headline is set in bold; the price in bold and in coral. The legal copy may be set in a small size, but it must always be legible: at least 14 pixels in banners and 20 pixels in stories. No more than three font sizes are used per asset.

## 6. Legibility and contrast

6.1. All text (headline, price, button and legal copy) must have a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 with its background, in line with WCAG level AA.

6.2. When text is placed over a busy photograph or one with light areas (sky, snow, sand or white façades), it goes on a solid or almost opaque band, with a minimum opacity of 85%, in teal, navy or cream.

6.3. Pale text (white, cream or light grey) on light backgrounds is not allowed, nor is text placed directly on busy photographs without a band.

6.4. Buttons have a coral background with white text, or a navy background with cream text.

## 7. Prices, discounts and legal copy

7.1. Prices are stated in euros and per person, using the formula «desde X €» (“from €X”). Discounts are stated as a percentage, for example «−30 %».

7.2. Every creative that shows a price or a discount must include the following legal copy, legibly and unaltered, in Spanish, because the ads target Spain:

«Precio final por persona. Plazas limitadas. Condiciones en contosoviajes.example/condiciones»

(“Final price per person. Limited places. Terms at contosoviajes.example/condiciones”.)

7.3. If the legal copy is missing, incomplete or altered, the creative is sent to Legal before it is published.

7.4. Assets that show no price or discount do not need the legal copy.

## 8. People and image rights

8.1. We prefer landscapes and destinations. If people appear, they must be seen from behind, in the distance or without a recognisable face.

8.2. If a creative shows an identifiable person, with their face visible, a signed image-rights release is mandatory, and Legal must verify it before publication. For this reason, these assets are always sent to Legal.

8.3. Images of minors are not used without the express authorisation of their parents or guardians, which Legal also verifies.

## 9. Formats

- **1:1** (1080 × 1080 pixels): feed posts.
- **9:16** (1080 × 1920 pixels): stories and vertical videos. The top and bottom 250 pixels, which are covered by the app interface, must be kept free of text.
- **1.91:1** (1200 × 628 pixels): banners and display ads.

Any other format requires the prior approval of the Brand team.

## 10. Review decision

Each creative receives one of these three decisions:

- **Publish** (*publicar*): it meets all the rules in these guidelines and can be published as is.
- **Fix** (*corregir*): Design must fix it before publication, because the logo is missing, distorted, recoloured or has effects; it uses off-palette colours; the contrast is insufficient or the band under the text is missing; or the format is not allowed.
- **Legal** (*legal*): the legal copy is missing from an asset with a price or discount, or an identifiable person appears.

Legal takes precedence: if an asset has both a legal issue and a design issue, it is sent to Legal, which will return it to Design with its instructions.
