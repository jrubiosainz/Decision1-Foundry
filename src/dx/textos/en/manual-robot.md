# Northwind Hogar RV-300 · Technical manual (excerpt)

## Chapter 7. Troubleshooting

The indicator light next to the power button shows the robot's status. Before calling technical support, check this table.

| Indicator or symptom | Probable cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Blinking red light; the robot stops after a few minutes | The main brush or the wheels are blocked by tangled hair or threads. This is very common in homes with pets. | The customer cleans the brush (section 7.1). |
| Steady red light | Battery fault | Technical support. Do not open the battery compartment. |
| Blinking orange light; the robot spins on the spot or does not move forward | Dirty or covered cliff sensors | Wipe the four sensors on the underside with a soft, dry cloth. |
| The robot does not charge on the dock | Dirty charging contacts on the robot or on the dock | Clean the contacts with a dry cloth and check that the dock is plugged in. |
| Strange noise, grinding or burning smell | Motor fault | Switch the robot off and contact technical support. |

## 7.1. Cleaning the main brush

1. Switch the robot off and turn it upside down on a soft surface.
2. Press the two tabs on the brush cover and remove it by hand.
3. Take out the main brush by pulling on its end.
4. Cut the tangled hair and threads with the cleaning tool provided or with scissors, and remove them.
5. Also remove any hair wound around the axles of the wheels and the side brush.
6. Refit the brush and the cover until you hear a click, and switch the robot on. The red light will stop blinking.

In homes with pets, we recommend cleaning the brush once a week.

## 7.2. Note for the customer service team

Cleaning the brush, the sensors and the charging contacts are maintenance tasks that are safe for the customer: they require no special tools and no opening of the device, and they solve most incidents. Before sending a technician, walk the customer through these steps. Each technical support visit costs the company about €90. Battery and motor faults always require a technician.
