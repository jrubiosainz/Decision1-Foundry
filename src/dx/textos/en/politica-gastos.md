# Fabrikam, S.A. travel and expense policy

Version 4.1 · In force since 1 January 2026 · Applies to all Fabrikam, S.A. staff (tax ID (NIF) A00000001, Calle Ficticia 12, 28000 Madrid).

## 1. Purpose and principles

This policy sets out which work expenses Fabrikam reimburses and how they must be supported. Only expenses that are necessary, reasonable and directly related to the company's business are reimbursed. When several equivalent options are available, employees shall choose the cheapest one. Each expense is declared in the expenses app with its receipt, category, amount, date and the project or cost centre to which it is charged. The automatic review system and the Finance team apply this policy exactly as written.

## 2. Receipts

2.1. Every expense requires a legible and complete receipt on which the date, the issuer (the business name and its tax ID), the items and the total amount can be clearly read.

2.2. If the receipt is illegible because it is blurred, faded, cut off or crumpled, and the date, issuer or amount cannot be read, the expense is not processed: the employee must provide the original or a legible copy.

2.3. A **full invoice** («factura completa») is one issued in the name of Fabrikam, S.A., with its tax ID A00000001 and its registered tax address. It is the only document that allows VAT to be recovered, and it is mandatory for hotels and for any expense over €50.

2.4. Tickets or simplified invoices without Fabrikam's details are accepted only for small expenses of up to €50; in that case, VAT cannot be recovered. If an expense over €50 is supported only by a simplified ticket, it goes to manual review so that the full invoice can be requested.

2.5. Train, plane or bus tickets issued in the employee's name are valid receipts in themselves, whatever their amount.

## 3. Declared details and deadlines

3.1. The declared amount must match the total amount on the receipt exactly, and the declared date must match the date on the receipt. Any difference, however small, sends the expense to manual review. Receipts write the date as day/month/year (07/10/2026 is 7 October 2026) and the expenses app as year-month-day (2026-10-07). For hotels, the expense date is the invoice date, which is the check-out day.

3.2. The declared category (transport, meals, accommodation, parking, supplies or other) must correspond to what was actually bought. If it does not, the expense goes to manual review.

3.3. Expenses must be submitted within a maximum of 30 calendar days of the expense date. Expenses submitted late are rejected, unless authorised in writing by the Finance Department.

3.4. A single expense may not be split across several claims to get around the limits of this policy.

## 4. Transport

4.1. **Taxi and ride-hailing (VTC):** allowed on business trips and work journeys, with the ticket or the app receipt as proof.

4.2. **Train:** tourist or standard class is reimbursed. First class requires the prior authorisation of the head of department.

4.3. **Plane:** economy class, booked through the company's travel agency and, whenever possible, at least seven days in advance.

4.4. **Private vehicle:** €0.26 per kilometre is paid, stating the origin, destination and purpose of the journey. Tolls are reimbursed with a receipt.

4.5. **Parking:** allowed on business trips and work visits, with a receipt. Fines and traffic penalties are not reimbursed.

4.6. **Public transport:** metro, bus and commuter train fares are reimbursed with the ticket or the transport card statement.

## 5. Meals

5.1. When employees are travelling or on a work journey away from their usual office, their meals are reimbursed up to €35 per person per meal.

5.2. Working breakfasts and coffees are reimbursed up to €10 per person, and a simplified ticket is sufficient.

5.3. Client meals are reimbursed up to €60 per person. The description must include the names and companies of all attendees.

5.4. Alcoholic drinks (wine, beer, spirits or cocktails) are not reimbursable. Any receipt that includes alcohol goes to manual review so that its amount can be deducted, even if the total is within the limit.

5.5. If the per-person limit is exceeded, the expense goes to manual review to adjust the amount; the excess is borne by the employee.

## 6. Accommodation

6.1. Hotels in Spain: up to €130 per night, breakfast included. Abroad, up to €180 per night.

6.2. Accommodation must always be supported by a full invoice in the name of Fabrikam, S.A., with its tax ID, so that the company can recover the VAT.

6.3. The minibar, films, laundry (except on trips of more than five nights) and other personal extras are not reimbursed.

6.4. If the hotel invoice includes dinner, the meal limit in section 5.1 applies to that dinner.

## 7. Supplies, equipment and personal expenses

7.1. Personal items, such as consumer electronics, headphones, clothing, gifts or leisure, are not reimbursable and are rejected.

7.2. IT equipment and office supplies over €50 must always be purchased through the IT procurement portal. They are never reimbursed through an expense claim: they are rejected, even if the receipt is correct.

7.3. Office supplies of up to €50 for a one-off need may be claimed as an expense, with a receipt.

## 8. Review outcome

Each expense receives one of these four decisions, applied in this order:

- **Request the original** (*pedir original*): the receipt cannot be read (date, issuer or amount), so the original or a legible copy is requested.
- **Reject** (*rechazar*): the expense is not reimbursable: personal items, IT equipment or supplies over €50 bought outside the procurement portal, or an expense submitted late.
- **Review** (*revisar*): something does not match or the amount must be adjusted: amount, date or category different from what was declared, alcohol, limits exceeded or a simplified ticket over €50.
- **Approve** (*aprobar*): the expense complies with this policy and matches what the employee declared.

## 9. Reimbursement and control

9.1. Approved expenses are paid with the following month's payroll. Expenses under review are resolved within a maximum of ten working days.

9.2. Finance may request the original receipts at any time and carry out sample audits. Misuse of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

9.3. Questions about this policy should be sent to gastos@fabrikam.example.
